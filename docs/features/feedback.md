# Feedback

On each video, a key tells the app what you want more or less of. The [preference learning](preference-learning.md) uses it, and every piece of feedback is undoable.

## What it does

- **No shorts**: hide shorts entirely.
- **More** or **less** like this: nudges the ranking of similar videos.
- **Always** or **never** from this person or channel: a hard rule, above any learned preference.
- **Not now**: hide the video without judging it.

## Open questions

- What does "like this" mean: the same source, the same theme, similar words in the transcript?
- Where does the list of hard rules live, to review and undo them later: a Settings section or a view of its own?
