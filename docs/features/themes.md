# Themes

A theme is a subject you want to follow or learn about: "tech conferences", "Rust", "developer experience". It is the entry point of the workflow: theme, then trusted sources, then a guided feed.

## What it does

- Create, rename and remove a theme; each is undoable.
- A theme holds its [sources](sources.md), its keywords, and the videos, learnings and notes gathered under it.
- The [feed](feed.md) shows one theme, or all of them.
- A video can belong to several themes.

## Open questions

- Is a theme only a filter over sources, or does it also match keywords in titles, descriptions and transcripts?
- Can a theme suggest its first sources from a search (see [Recommendations](recommendations.md))?
