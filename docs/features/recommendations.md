# Recommendations

Beyond your sources, the app can suggest new ones, from a search you make or from its own suggestions, never mixed into the feed without your say.

## What it does

- Search YouTube for a theme and propose channels and creators to add as [sources](sources.md).
- Suggest videos and sources from your history and [learned preferences](preference-learning.md) when [connected](accounts.md).
- Keep suggestions apart from the feed, in their own list, until you accept one.

## Open questions

- A search costs 100 units of the daily YouTube API quota: how many can the app afford, and when?
- Can the [references](references.md) found in videos feed the suggestions?
