# Sources

A source is a channel or a creator you trust about a [theme](themes.md). Following a few of them, and nothing else, is the heart of the app.

## What it does

- Add a channel by its URL, handle or a fuzzy search.
- Add a creator, a person rather than a channel: their videos appear whether they publish on their own channel or are mentioned or featured in someone else's (a conference talk, an interview, a podcast episode).
- Mark a source always shown or never shown (see [Feedback](feedback.md)).
- Import sources from your YouTube subscriptions once [connected](accounts.md), choosing which to keep.

## Open questions

- How is a creator recognised in another channel's video: the title, the description, the transcript, or a list of aliases you give?
- Does a creator mentioned in passing count, or only one who appears?
