# Feed

The feed shows only the videos you asked for, from your [sources](sources.md) and [themes](themes.md), organized, and ends there: no "next video", no sidebar, no autoplay.

## What it does

- Lists new videos by theme, newest or most relevant first, with keys to move, open and act.
- Hides shorts entirely when you choose to, globally or per source.
- Marks watched, skipped and saved videos.
- Shows, next to each video, its [summary](analysis.md) when there is one, to help you decide whether to watch it.
- Plays a video without YouTube's recommendations around it, or opens it on YouTube.
- Every fuzzy search covers titles, sources, summaries and notes.
